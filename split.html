Upper / Lower Split

4 lifting days, 6:00–7:00 am. Each exercise shows the muscles it works, three form cues, and a video link showing how to do it.

How to progress

  • Weeks 1–2: use light weights and learn the movement. Watch the video before your first session.
  • Add weight once you hit the top of the rep range on every set: +2.5 kg for upper body, +5 kg for legs.
  • End each set with 1–2 reps still in the tank. Bad form means the weight is too heavy.
  • Warm up 5–8 minutes, then do 2 light sets of the first exercise.
  • Write down the weight and reps of your top set so you know what to beat next time.
  • Week 8: deload. Same exercises, half the sets, lighter weight.
